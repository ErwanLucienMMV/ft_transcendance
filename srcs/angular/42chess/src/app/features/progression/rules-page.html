Progression

How progression works

Your rating measures your strength against other players. Experience (XP) rewards everything you play: it raises your level and never goes down.

Rating and leaderboard

  • Every account starts at 1200. Only rated games against other players change it.
  • A win against a stronger player earns more than a win against a weaker one (Elo, K = 32).
  • The leaderboard lists every player with at least one rated game, best rating first. Equal ratings share a rank.
