42 Chess is an online chess site built by a team of students at 42 school as part of the ft_transcendence project. This page explains which personal data the site keeps, why, who can see it and how long it is kept. It applies to every page of the site and to its realtime connection.

Data we collect

  • Account: your username, your email address and a password. The password is never stored as typed: we keep only an Argon2id hash of it. If you sign in with Google or GitHub, we keep the identifier that provider gives your account instead of a password.
  • Email verification: the date your address was confirmed, and a one-time code (stored as a hash) that expires after 30 minutes.
  • Two-factor authentication, if you turn it on: the secret used to check your codes and a count of failed attempts, to block repeated guessing.
  • Profile: the address of your avatar picture, if you set one, and your display preferences.
  • Play: the games you play (players, moves, clocks, result and dates), your Elo rating and its changes after each rated game, your win, draw and loss counts, and your daily puzzle attempts.
  • Social: your friends and friend requests, whether you are online or in a game, and when you were last seen.
  • Sessions: one record for each signed-in browser, with its expiry date and whether you signed it out.

We do not collect your real name, your address, payment details or your location. We do not use advertising, tracking or third-party analytics.

Why we use it

  • To create your account, sign you in and keep it secure (verification, two-factor, sessions).
  • To run games in real time, rate them fairly and keep your game history.
  • To show friends when you are online and let them challenge you.
  • To keep the service working: we measure request counts, response times and the number of games in progress. These measurements never contain user or game identifiers.

The legal basis is the agreement you accept when creating an account (the Terms of Service) and our interest in keeping the site secure.

Who can see it

  • Everyone, including visitors who are not signed in: your username, avatar, Elo rating, rating history, number of games, whether you are online, your finished games and their moves, and the games you are playing now (anyone can watch a game in progress).
  • Your friends: when you come online, go offline or start a game.
  • Only you: your email address, preferences, friend requests, puzzle attempts and sessions.
  • Nobody: your password and two-factor secret are never sent back by the site, not even to you.

We never sell or rent your data.

Other services involved

  • Google and GitHub, only if you choose to sign in with them. They tell us your account identifier and email address; their own privacy policies apply on their pages.
  • Our email provider, which delivers the verification email to your address.

Cookies and browser storage

The site sets a single cookie, and it is strictly necessary:

  • refresh_token keeps you signed in after a reload. It cannot be read by scripts (httpOnly), is only sent to the sign-in service of this site (SameSite=Strict) and expires after seven days, or as soon as you sign out.

Your short-lived access token stays in the page's memory only. While you solve a daily puzzle, the tab's session storage remembers which attempt is in progress, so a reload can resume it; it is erased when you close the tab. No consent banner is needed because nothing else is stored.

How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, games and ratings: as long as your account exists.
  • Sessions: they stop working after seven days or when you sign out.
  • Email verification codes: 30 minutes. Two-factor sign-in challenges: 5 minutes.

If you ask for your account to be deleted, the team erases your account data. Finished games stay in your opponents' history, but no longer show your username.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you can ask to see the data we hold about you, to correct it, to receive a copy of it, to have it deleted, or object to how it is used. Signing out ends the session of that browser at once. To use these rights, contact the team members through the 42 school intranet; we answer within one month. You may also complain to the CNIL, the French data protection authority (www.cnil.fr).

Security

The site is served over HTTPS only. Passwords are hashed with Argon2id, repeated wrong two-factor codes are blocked, and every request and realtime connection checks that its session is still valid.

Children

The site is meant for people aged 15 or older. Younger players need a parent's or guardian's permission to create an account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. A change that affects how your data is used will be announced on the site before it applies.
